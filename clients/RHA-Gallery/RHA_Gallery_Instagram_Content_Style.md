# RHA Gallery — Instagram Content Style

> Organic Instagram style reference, based on a review of @rha_gallery (Nov 2026). Use alongside `RHA_Gallery_Brief_Style_Guide.md` (ads brief standard) so organic and ads content stay consistent. Live doc: https://claude.ai/artifact/CvKBkcAqjdWjTJLhzM3nLh

## Account snapshot

RHA Gallery sells classroom decoration and educational posters to Indonesian teachers, and its feed reads as a friendly teacher's helper first, shop second. Written as the vendor reference for briefing RHA content, based on a review of [@rha_gallery](https://www.instagram.com/rha_gallery/) and three recent posts.

- **Positioning:** "Poster Edukasi Kelas Kreatif" with the tagline "Inspiring Room, Inspiring Study"
- **Audience:** school and bimbel (tutoring) teachers, mostly SD level, addressed as Bapak/Ibu Guru
- **Scale:** 624 posts, 40.7k followers, category Shopping & retail
- **Sales path:** every post points to Shopee, Tokopedia, TikTok Shop via desty.page; sister account @rha_catalog holds the catalog
- **Highlights:** Review, Penipuan (scam warning), Testimoni, Realpict, Shopee, Tokopedia, FAQ — trust-building comes before the sale
- **What lands:** relatable teacher-life text posts drew about 485 likes and classroom-corner tips about 213, while product-led bundle reels drew single digits

## Visual style

The look is bright, pastel and "classroom-cute": a decorated room as the set, products in use, and big playful headline text on every cover.

| Element | How RHA does it |
| --- | --- |
| Palette | Soft pastels — baby blue, mint, pink, lilac, butter yellow — on white or light walls; product colors (blue-yellow, astronaut navy) pop against it |
| Set | Real or staged classrooms: pegboard walls, bunting flags, bookshelves, pojok baca corners, whiteboards |
| People | A smiling hijab-wearing presenter pointing at or holding the product; the hand-to-product shot is the signature |
| Cover text | Bold rounded display font with white outline and drop shadow, in navy or blue, plus sparkle and star doodles |
| Carousel covers | Clean white top with small RHA Gallery logo, purple or magenta headline bar (e.g. "4 Hiasan Gantung"), photo below |
| Text posts | Plain white card, purple headline, short black body, one simple doodle (e.g. a crying blob) |
| Before/after | Raw, worn real classroom photo with "POV: Guru mulai kepikiran makeover kelas" to set up the transformation |
| Format mix | Reels for product demos, carousels for tips and lists, single text cards for relatable moments |

## Voice & captions

RHA talks like a warm fellow teacher: casual Bahasa Indonesia, empathetic, encouraging, never hard-sell.

- **Tone:** santai and supportive; particles like "lho", "yaa", "yuk", "biar", "nggak"; addresses readers as "kamu" or "Bapak/Ibu Guru"
- **Humor:** self-aware teacher humor in headlines ("Setelah ngajar: 1% sabar, 99% butuh healing")
- **Structure:** 1) hook that names a teacher feeling or classroom problem, 2) one or two short lines of value or product benefit, 3) a CTA question or save prompt
- **CTAs:** "Simpan postingan ini", "Coba komen…", "Kalau di kelasmu, papan ini bakal dipakai buat apa dulu?"
- **Length:** 2–4 short paragraphs; emotional posts can run longer
- **Emoji:** 1–2 per paragraph, at line ends — ✨ 🏫 📝 🥹 ❤️ 🌷 💬
- **Product mentions:** named naturally inside a benefit ("Dengan Bundling Papan Tulis Kuning Biru dari RHA Gallery…")
- **Hashtags:** 5–10 at the end — always #rhagallery or #RHAGallery plus #GuruIndonesia and topic tags like #DekorasiKelas, #InspirasiKelas, #MotivasiGuru, #GuruBimbel

## Content pillars

Four pillars carry the feed; lead with the first two for reach and let the last two convert.

| Pillar | Format | Example from the feed | Job |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Teacher life & motivation | Single text card | "Kabar Buruknya / Kabar Baiknya" | Reach, comments, shares |
| Classroom tips & ideas | Carousel or reel | "4 Sudut di Kelas yang Sering Dilupakan Guru" | Saves, authority |
| Product in use | Reel with presenter | "Sudut Kelas Tema Astronot", "Belajar Perilaku Baik Lewat Dekorasi Kelas" | Desire, click to shop |
| Makeover & trust | POV before/after, testimonials, real pics | "POV: Guru mulai kepikiran makeover kelas" | Proof, purchase confidence |

## Do's and don'ts

**Do**

- Open every caption with a teacher feeling or classroom problem
- Show the product on a real wall, with a hand or presenter for scale
- Keep covers bright pastel with one bold outlined headline
- End with a save prompt or a question teachers can answer
- Tag #rhagallery and #GuruIndonesia every time

**Don't**

- Post flat product shots with price-first copy
- Use dark, muted or corporate palettes
- Write formal or stiff Indonesian
- Stack more than about 10 hashtags or clutter the cover with text
