## 2. Brand Positioning

- **Who it's for:** teachers, schools (TK/SD/SMP), parents and students. Secondary market is home décor (kids' rooms, photo walls, cafés).
- **Core idea:** educational and decorative wall pieces that make classrooms neat, cheerful and good for learning.
- **The store's own selling points:** "Desain Elegan, Exclusive" and "Murah Banget" (elegant, exclusive designs at very low prices).
- **Brand voice in seller replies:** warm and grateful, with emojis (🥰💛). Talks about "desain edukatif yang bermakna dan berkualitas" (meaningful, high-quality educational designs) and inspiring learning spaces.
- **Seasonal hooks:** new school year décor ("Dekor Kelas Tahun Ajaran Baru 2026"), Islamic-themed collections and a "Koleksi Terbaru" (new arrivals) section.

## 3. Product Categories

The store sorts its catalogue into these groups:

1. **📅 Planner & Jadwal Sekolah (school planners and schedules):** class schedule, class organisation structure, cleaning duty roster, attendance and daily/weekly/monthly planners. Sold singly or in 2-pc and 3-pc bundles. Also includes a 2025 calendar.
2. **📏 Papan Tulis & Aktivitas (wooden writing boards):** schedule and organisation boards with a wooden rail, plus the "Paket Lengkap Upgrade Papan Tulis" bundle.
3. **🎨 Poster Dekorasi Kelas (classroom décor posters):** square quote posters, wooden-frame posters, President and Vice President photos, solar system, adab (etiquette), wayang, digestive system, math, signs and prohibitions, 7 Wonders of the World.
4. **📚 Poster Pendidikan Sekolah (school education posters):** maps of ASEAN, Indonesia and the world, traditional houses and costumes, national heroes, Pancasila and UUD 1945, anti-bullying, class rules, school culture and code of conduct.
5. **🕌 Islamic content:** Islamic education posters, Rukun Iman and Rukun Islam, daily prayers, Islamic calligraphy, Islamic quotes and the "Bingkai Tema Whimsical Islami" frame line.
6. **🌟 Poster Motivasi & Kata Bijak (motivation and quotes):** wooden-frame quotes, quote bundles and inspirational quotes.
7. **🖼️ Hiasan Dinding Gantung (hanging wall signs):** door and room signs such as Pojok Baca (reading corner), Welcome to Library, Welcome to Our Class, OSIS and class rules sets.
8. **🔤 Poster Alfabet & Angka (alphabet and numbers):** includes class-name letters such as "KELAS 4B" with custom colours.
9. **🛠️ Custom Gambar & Desain Khusus (custom designs):** custom A3+ posters, custom text signs and custom photo frames in 20×30 and 40×60.
10. **Banners (100×50 cm):** periodic table, syllable chart (suku kata), President and Vice President.

## 5. Materials & Specs by Product Type

### Wipe & Clean Planners (A3+)
- Size: A3+ (32×48 cm)
- Waterproof and reusable: write, wipe and write again
- High-quality print
- Wooden rail 1.8 cm thick, comes with a hanging rope
- **Marker included** (reviews say an eraser is included too)
- Many design variants (e.g. 14 motifs on the best-seller: APSB1–APSB14)
- Shopee board type: "Paper Planner"

### Wooden Boards (Papan Tulis Kayu)
- Same wipe-and-reuse, waterproof surface as the planners
- Sizes 30×40 or 40×60
- Hanging rope included, free extra bubble wrap

### Posters (A3+)
- Thick 380 gsm paper (spec sheet lists Ivory 310)
- Rounded, non-sharp corners, which makes them safe for kids
- **Glossy lamination:** doesn't fade, waterproof, easy to wipe clean
- High-resolution print, size 32×48 cm
- "Aesthetic & colourful"

### Framed Sets (e.g. President set)
- Backing: 6 mm MDF
- Frame: 3 cm fiber polyframe
- Comes with a metal hook, ready to hang
- Glossy lamination, high-resolution print
- Glass or acrylic cover included
- Pack: 3 pcs of 20×30 cm (10R)
- Warranty: 1-month manufacturer warranty

### Banners
- 100×50 cm, HD print
- Store highlights quality material, easy to hang and cheerful designs

## 6. Packaging & After-Sales

- **Free extra bubble wrap** on every order.
- Buyers of **one item** are asked to add the paid cardboard box or bubble-wrap add-on to avoid dents. The store says shipping damage is not its responsibility.
- Orders of **2 or more items** automatically get a cardboard box and bubble wrap.
- For a problem (wrong or damaged item), buyers chat the seller with a photo and an unboxing video.
- Some variants are **pre-order**, and one review mentioned the wait.

---

## 7. What Customers Say

**Praise that comes up again and again:**

- **Very safe, tidy packing.** This is the most common compliment.
- **Bigger than expected:** "kirain kecil" (I thought it'd be small).
- **Bright, vivid colours** and cute or appealing designs.
- **Waterproof and lasts** long enough for kids to use for a long time.
- **Free marker and eraser** feel like great value at about Rp18K.
- **Thick material** that doesn't bend ("tebal, tidak mleyot").

**Watch-outs:**

- Pre-order waiting time.
- One buyer said marker writing rubs off a little too easily.
